SECTION 06

Product Story

Selected products deserve more than one photo. Material, craft, comfort and dimensions — told as you scroll.

01

MATERIAL

A linen-blend upholstery over a kiln-dried solid beech frame. In production, every claim here would link to the supplier specification sheet EMKAN provides.

02

CRAFT

Reinforced joinery, hand-finished edges, upholstery pulled and stapled by hand. Detail photography is what turns a commodity into a considered purchase.

03

COMFORT

Multi-density foam with a soft top layer and fiber-filled back cushions — support that keeps its shape. Comfort copy reduces returns and hesitation.

04

DIMENSIONS

W 220 × D 95 × H 85 cm, seat height 45 cm. Publishing dimensions answers the questions customers usually send one message at a time on WhatsApp.

SECTION 08

The Digital Sales System

What this website would connect — a full path from first discovery to a lead in EMKAN's hands.

  1. 01Customer discovers product

    Search, category or filter leads them to the right piece.

  2. 02Views full details

    Gallery, dimensions, materials, price and availability.

  3. 03Cart / WhatsApp inquiry

    Two doors: demo cart now, real WhatsApp in production.

  4. 04Order or inquiry submitted

    Structured data instead of scattered voice notes.

  5. 05Business receives the lead

    Product, name and intent arrive together.

  6. 06Customer follow-up

    Quote, visit booking or delivery — tracked to close.

Concept flow — infrastructure (payments, inventory, CRM) is scoped for the production build.

SECTION 10

Why This Version?

The principles behind every decision in this demo.

  1. 01Product-first

    Customers see furniture before they read company history.

  2. 02Easy discovery

    Categories, search and filters reduce the path to the right piece.

  3. 03Strong visual presentation

    Large imagery, generous spacing, clear hierarchy.

  4. 04Searchable catalog

    The whole range, findable in seconds.

  5. 05Mobile-first

    Touch interactions redesigned, not shrunk.

  6. 06Conversion-focused

    Every section has one clear next step.

  7. 07Ready for future e-commerce infrastructure

    Catalog structure, cart and product data model are already shaped for payments, inventory and checkout when EMKAN is ready.

FINAL

THIS IS NOT THE FINAL WEBSITE.

It is a demonstration of what EMKAN's digital commerce experience could become.

The final website would be designed around EMKAN's actual brand identity, real products, real photography, real pricing, real categories, real customer journey and real business requirements.
